🎬 Netflix Data Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

I'm excited to showcase my latest Data Analytics project: Netflix Data Analysis using Power BI!

In this project, I analyzed Netflix content and customer ratings datasets to understand customer viewing behavior, content popularity, subscription trends, and audience preferences.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.

Identify the Top 10 most-watched shows and movies.

Analyze customer demographics based on age, gender, and country.

Compare watch time across Basic, Standard, and Premium subscriptions.

Understand customer ratings and content preferences.

Identify popular genres across different countries.

Analyze customer engagement and retention patterns.

Explore high-rated and low-rated content.
